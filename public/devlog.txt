TREMOROIDS
==========

An old-school sideways scroller with a graphical style similar to asteroids.
Rocks tumble from the sky and crash into the ground, causing debris and craters.
The player drives a tank across the landscape, trying to avoid getting stuck by
shooting the rocks and the ground to clear a path.
Some rocks release cannon upgrades for more interesting shot types.
When the player gets stuck or squashed by a rock, the game is over.


Collision physics
Rocks are irregular polygons and may be rotating as they fall.
For a first aproximation, the maximum radius of the rock can be used to check collisions.
But inside this radius, we must be more precise:
1. rock:rock collisions
For each point on Rock A, check if it is inside the boundary of rock B.
And then for each point on Rock B, check if it is inside the boundary of A
If any of these tests is true, we collide

2. rock:landscape collisions
For each point on the rock, find the landscape point immdiately left and right of it.
check if the rock point lies below the line drawn between those points
