TREMOROIDS
==========

An old-school sideways scroller with a graphical style similar to asteroids.
Rocks tumble from the sky and crash into the ground, causing debris and craters.
The player drives a tank across the landscape, trying to avoid getting stuck by
shooting the rocks and the ground to clear a path.
Some rocks release cannon upgrades for more interesting shot types.
When the player gets stuck or squashed by a rock, the game is over.


Collision physics
Rocks are irregular polygons and may be rotating as they fall.
For a first aproximation, the maximum radius of the rock can be used to check collisions.
But inside this radius, we must be more precise:
1. rock:rock collisions
Rocks do not collide with each other - it's a bit too busy

2. rock:landscape collisions
For each of the points in the outline of the rock (corrected for its present rotation),
calculate the height of the landscape. If that point on the landscape lies within the outline
of the rock, then we trigger a collision.

3. tank:rock collisions
If the tank bounding box collides with a rock, the tank explodes.

Craters & debris
Rocks above a threshold size create a crater that is based on the outline of the bottom 
half of the rock. The crater outline is averaged with the existing landscape profile.
The original rock also fractures into a random number of rocks from 1 to 5 smaller rocks. 
(A rock that "splits" into a single rock, is really just bouncing, but the original rock is destroyed 
and a new, smaller one is created.)

Rocks below the crater threshold will use the top half of their outline to raise the outline of
the landscape. This simulates debris accumulating on the surface.
